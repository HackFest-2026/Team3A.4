AI-Powered Energy Monitoring and Automatic Protection System

Project Overview
GridGuardian-AI is a smart energy monitoring and protection system that uses an ESP32 microcontroller to monitor electrical parameters, detect abnormal conditions, and support automatic protection of electrical equipment.

Problem Statement
Electrical systems may experience voltage fluctuations, excessive current, and abnormal power consumption. These conditions can damage electrical equipment and cause energy wastage. A smart system is needed to monitor electrical parameters and provide timely alerts.

Proposed Solution
GridGuardian-AI aims to monitor voltage, current, power, and energy using suitable sensors connected to the ESP32. The system checks measurements against configured safety limits and can trigger alerts and a protection relay when abnormal conditions occur. AI-based anomaly detection is a planned feature.

Objectives
Monitor electrical parameters in real time.
Measure voltage, current, power, and energy.
Identify abnormal electrical conditions.
Provide alerts when configured limits are exceeded.
Support automatic protection using a relay.
Explore AI-based analysis for detecting unusual energy usage.
Key Technologies
Microcontroller: ESP32
AI and Dashboard: Planned features; implementation details will be updated.
Working Principle
Sensors measure electrical parameters.
The ESP32 reads and processes the sensor data.
The measurements are compared with configured safety limits.
When an abnormal condition is detected, the system can activate an alert and protection relay.
AI-based analysis may be added to identify unusual energy consumption patterns.
Project Status

The project is currently in the planning and development stage. Hardware integration, programming, testing, and additional features will be documented as development progresses.
